# 🚕 Uber Trip Analysis in New York City

📍 A data-driven exploratory analysis of Uber trips in NYC, using Python and visualization tools to uncover usage patterns, ride volume trends, and geographical hotspots. This project helps understand when, where, and how Uber usage peaks across different timeframes.

---

## 🧠 Objective

Analyze Uber NYC trip data to identify trends related to:
- Ride volume
- Time-based demand fluctuations
- Geographic pickup/drop-off hotspots

---

## 📊 Key Features

<details>
  <summary>🛣️ Ride Volume & Trend Analysis</summary>

- Distribution of rides over hours, weekdays, and months  
- Peak vs off-peak periods  
- Trend shifts across time frames (weekends vs weekdays)

</details>

<details>
  <summary>📍 Geographical Insights</summary>

- Popular pickup & drop-off zones (e.g. Manhattan, near Penn Station)
- Heatmaps of demand concentration
- Zone-based ride density tracking

</details>

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- **Language**: Python  
- **Libraries**: Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn  
- **IDE**: Jupyter Notebook  
- **Visualization Techniques**: Heatmaps, Line Charts, Time-based grouping  
- **Dataset**: [Uber NYC Trip Data](https://github.com/fivethirtyeight/uber-tlc-foil-response)

---

## 📈 Results & Insights

- 🚦 Ride demand peaks during morning & evening rush hours
- 🗺️ High-traffic zones include Manhattan and transit hubs
- 🗓️ Weekends show different behavior compared to weekdays (more evenly distributed demand)
